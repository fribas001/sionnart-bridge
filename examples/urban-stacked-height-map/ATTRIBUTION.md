\# Munich scene attribution



The city geometry in this example is based on the Munich example environment

distributed with Sionna RT.



The underlying city data originate from OpenStreetMap.



© OpenStreetMap contributors



OpenStreetMap data are available under the Open Data Commons Open Database

License (ODbL).



This repository adds the SionnaRT-Bridge configuration, transmitter and

receiver setup, stacked-height radio-map results, Geometry Nodes

visualization, materials, camera setup, and animation.
