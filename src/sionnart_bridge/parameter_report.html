Parameter analysis

SionnaRT-Bridge · geometry inputs and channel response
Channel metrics use the first transmit–receive antenna pair. “Sum of path powers” is 10 log₁₀(Σ |aₖ|²), in dB; it is not received power in dBm or the beamformed full-array gain.

 

Settings that differ between runs

These differences are recorded inputs. A gain difference cannot be assigned to one setting when several changed.

Inputs and variation

Descriptive variation over the sampled frames. Standard deviation and correlation do not establish uncertainty, statistical significance or convergence.

Definitions and limits

Vegetation measurements use final evaluated meshes with vegetation radio materials. Leaf mesh islands are count estimates; supplied positive face IDs provide a separate tagged count. Automatic one-sided area uses full area for open islands and half surface area for closed islands. Leaf area density and leaf area index labelled “bounding-box estimate” divide by the world-axis-aligned vegetation envelope volume or its XY footprint, not a measured canopy or plot. Link density clips leaf surfaces to a square corridor between TX and RX and divides by the entire corridor volume, including empty space. Its width is recorded in the settings. Surface crossings refer to the straight segment, not traced propagation paths. Link measurements follow the selected TX/RX pair.

Statistics omit missing or non-finite X/Y values and show how many records were omitted. Frames with no paths have missing gain/delay values, rather than artificial −600 dB values. Count and line-of-sight values remain available.

Mean, median and standard deviation use the displayed units, including dB. Δ is the last valid frame value minus the first valid frame value. No percentage change is computed for dB. Population standard deviation describes this set of sampled frames. Variation is flagged when max − min exceeds max(10⁻⁹, 10⁻⁷ × largest absolute value).

Pearson r uses matched numeric X/Y observations, requires at least three observations and variation in both axes, and is descriptive only. Repeated X values remain separate frame observations; they are not treated as independent uncertainty trials. Lines follow frame order. Hover over points to see frame and value.

Group material names record node assignments. They do not alone establish the dielectric properties applied by the solver. Mesh volume requires closed, consistently oriented surfaces. Several varying inputs can confound a single-parameter interpretation.
